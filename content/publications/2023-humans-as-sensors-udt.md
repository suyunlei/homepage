abstract: "While urban digital twins (UDTs) have rapidly gained traction as tools for city planning and decision-making, prevailing approaches focus heavily on physical infrastructure while overlooking the human dimension. This paper proposes a conceptual framework that explicitly positions humans as sensors within urban digital twins, capturing dynamic person–environment interactions to bridge physical, social, and virtual urban systems. The framework is demonstrated through a case study on outdoor walking comfort at the National University of Singapore (NUS) campus, highlighting the social value of participatory human-sensing data in UDTs."

### 核心研究发现 / Key Takeaways

- **补齐城市数字孪生中的「人本维度」**：针对现有 UDT 过度关注物理基础设施建模的不足，提出将人类感知纳入系统闭环的「人即传感器」物理–社会–虚拟融合框架。
- **NUS 校园步行舒适度验证与最佳论文奖**：在新加坡国立大学校园室外步行舒适度场景完成实证验证，获 **2023 年第 18 届国际 3D GeoInfo 会议最佳论文奖（Best Paper Award）**。
