abstract: "Urban expansion has always been a topic of great concern. The purpose of this study is to explore land use change and the types of urban expansion in Shenzhen from 1995 to 2015, and to indicate the driving factors of this change, so as to provide a paradigm for other similar studies. By analysing the landscape expansion index and the correlation coefficient between urban area and various factors in Shenzhen, the following conclusions are obtained: 1) The main changes of land use types are the decrease of cultivated land and the increase of urban land. The land cover type changed most dramatically from 2000 to 2005, and the urban land transformed from cultivated land and grassland occupied most of the area. 2) Analysis shows that during the 20 years from 1995 to 2015, the main expansion type is edge-expansion. In detail, during the period from 1995 to 2010, the proportion of infilling has been increasing, while that of the outlying has been decreasing. After 2010, the urban area of Shenzhen increased slightly. Besides, according to the landscape expansion index, Shenzhen experienced dramatic urban expansion from 2000 to 2005. 3) Education and population growth are the main factors of urban growth in Shenzhen, which is reflected in the strongest correlation between university enrolment rate and urban area."

### 核心研究发现 / Key Takeaways

- **土地利用转移（1995–2015）**：深圳耕地面积由 `700 km²` 下降至 `480 km²`，建设用地由 `420 km²` 增长至 `850 km²`；2000–2005 年间转入幅度最为剧烈，新增建设用地主要来源于耕地与草地。
- **景观扩张形态与速率**：基于 **LEI** 与 **UGI** 分析表明，20 年间以边缘式扩张（Edge-expansion，累计 `278.7 km²`）为主导，1995–2010 年填充式扩张（Infilling）比例持续上升、飞地式（Outlying）下降；UGI 在 **2000–2005 年达到峰值 `58.84 km²/a`**，2010 年后受土地资源约束降至 `1.40 km²/a`。
- **驱动力定量排序**：Pearson 相关分析显示，高校在校生规模（`r = 0.934`）与常住人口（`r = 0.912`）与城市建设用地扩张的相关性高于人均 GDP（`0.827`）、固定资产投资（`0.825`）及货运量（`0.814`）等单一经济指标。
