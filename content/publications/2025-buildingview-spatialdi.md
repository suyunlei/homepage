abstract: "Urban building exteriors are increasingly important in urban analytics, driven by advancements in Street View Imagery and its integration with urban research. Multimodal Large Language Models (LLMs) offer powerful tools for urban annotation, enabling deeper insights into urban environments. We propose BuildingView, a novel approach that integrates high-resolution visual data from Google Street View with spatial information from OpenStreetMap via the Overpass API to improve the accuracy of urban building exterior data, identify critical indicators for energy efficiency, environmental sustainability, and human-centric design, and systematically categorize them using multimodal LLM annotation. Validated with data from New York City, Amsterdam, and Singapore, the resulting database supports informed decision-making in urban planning, architectural design, and environmental policy."

### 核心研究发现 / Key Takeaways

- **空间矢量与街景视觉融合**：通过 Overpass API 结合 OpenStreetMap 建筑空间信息与高分辨率街景影像，解决传统建筑外立面语义属性匮乏与数据碎片化问题。
- **跨洲际三城实证验证**：在纽约、阿姆斯特丹与新加坡建立标准化建筑立面属性库并开源流程，为城市建筑能效评估、环境可持续性与以人为本的立面设计提供数据底座。
